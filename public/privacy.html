Tap Genius Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Tap Genius is a multiplayer party game played on phones. This policy explains what the game (the website at tap.alkouri.com and the Android and iOS apps) does with information. We keep it minimal: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

  • A photo of your face (selfie). The game asks for camera access so your face can appear as your player avatar. The photo is sent to our game server and shown to the other players in your game.
  • A nickname that you type, shown to other players.
  • Your game activity (answers, scores, taps) while a game is running, to run the game.

How long it is kept

Photos, nicknames and scores are held only in the server's memory while your game room exists. They are never written to a database or to disk. They are deleted when the game room closes, and in any case after at most 2 hours of inactivity. Restarting the server also clears them.

Your device remembers your last nickname, photo and a temporary reconnect token (in local storage) so you can rejoin quickly after a dropped connection. You can clear this by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking SDKs, or cookies.
  • We do not require an account, email address, or any real name.
  • We do not use your photo for anything except showing it to the players in your game.

Children

Tap Genius includes trivia for young children (ages 2 and up), who play with a parent or guardian's help. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above, which is temporary, is visible only to the players in the same game, and is automatically deleted. Taking a photo is something a parent or guardian can skip or help with; deny the camera permission and no photo is taken. A parent or guardian who has questions or wants to ask about data can contact us below.

Who can see your photo

Only the other players in the same game room, during the game. Rooms are joined from a live list of open games, so host your game only when the people you want to play with are ready.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS and secure WebSockets. Because data is held only in memory and deleted quickly, there is no stored archive to leak.
